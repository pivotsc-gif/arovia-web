Understanding Home Care Options

Two Distinct Types of Home Care at Arovia

To help families choose the right level of support, Arovia clearly separates everyday non-clinical caregiving from professional clinical nursing care.

Personal Daily Assistance

Caregivers (Attendants)

Caregivers provide non-medical, personal day-to-day assistance for older adults, patients with limited mobility, or individuals recovering who need practical help with ordinary daily living activities.

Assistance with bathing, dressing, grooming, and personal hygiene
Meal feeding assistance and fluid intake support
Help with walking, bed-to-chair transfers, and fall prevention
Attentive companionship, emotional reassurance, and routine support
Learn More About Caregiver Support
Clinical Healthcare

Registered Nurses

Registered nurses deliver specialized clinical healthcare procedures at home for post-surgical patients, individuals requiring medical procedures, and patients under ongoing medical supervision.

Physician-prescribed injections and intravenous (IV) infusion therapy
Sterile post-operative wound dressings and bed-sore prevention
Catheter care, Ryle's tube feeding assistance, and routine lines care
Vital sign monitoring, clinical observations, and updates to the doctor
Learn More About Clinical Nursing

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers to common questions about Arovia's healthcare coordination and services.

What is Arovia and what services do you provide?

Arovia is a healthcare coordination service based in Guntur. We help patients and families arrange six essential services: home care (caregivers and registered nurses), patient ambulance transport, medical equipment, pharmacy medicines, clinic services, and home lab test collections. We handle the coordination so your family doesn't have to manage multiple contacts.

How do I arrange a service with Arovia?

Just call our helpline. Tell us about your patient's condition and what kind of help you need. Our team will listen carefully, verify availability, and arrange the right service for you.

Do I need an online account or mobile app to use Arovia?

No. You don't need an app, account, or online registration. You speak directly with our team over the phone, and we share service details and confirmations with you on WhatsApp.

How do I know whether my patient needs a caregiver or a nurse?

If your loved one needs help with daily living activities—such as bathing, dressing, eating, walking safely, and companionship—a caregiver is the right choice. If they require clinical procedures like injections, IV infusions, sterile wound dressings, or catheter care, a registered nurse is required under doctor's guidance. You can also call us to discuss your patient's routine.

How does medical equipment ordering and setup work?

We coordinate hospital-grade equipment including electric ICU beds, vital monitors, and mobility aids. You can review specifications on our website and call our medical desk to discuss home delivery, setup guidance, or institutional export requirements.

How quickly can an ambulance or patient transit be arranged?

For non-emergency transfers, hospital discharges, or routine dialysis visits, call our dispatch line directly. Our coordinators verify vehicle availability, stretcher requirements, and oxygen needs right away, scheduling pickup to match your timing.
